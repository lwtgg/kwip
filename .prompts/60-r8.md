# Task 60, round eight — the parity oracle's systematic bias

`/tmp/60-review7.log`, approve with nits. The layout is done. Every ratio passes at every width, engine and DPR
(worst 20.5 %), hero fill is 82.4 % or more everywhere including 320/390 and 200 % text, nothing clips, and there are
no regressions. The remaining findings are all about the parity assertion, and they are one root cause:

- Finding 1: DPR 2 headroom is 0.022 px (WebKit 0.478) and 0.052 px (Chromium 0.448) against the 0.5 limit. The
  error is **systematic**, about 0.8-0.9 device px of outward AA/gamma bias, constant per engine — not noise. A small
  renderer change fails the assertion with no layout change.
- Finding 2: the WebKit paint-snap model change buys the DPR 2 pass by 0.003-0.026 px; without it 4/6 cells fail at
  0.503-0.526.
- Finding 3: the check cannot see one device pixel at DPR 4 and is weakened by inflating the score ink, so it is a
  >= ~0.4-0.5 px detector, not a sub-pixel one.

The bias is outward because the decoder takes the first non-background pixel, so it measures the AA fringe, not the
geometric edge. Fix the oracle rather than the tolerance:

1. **Threshold the ink at coverage, not presence.** Convert the decoded row to alpha against the plate colour and take
   the edge at a fixed coverage (start at 50 %, sweep 25-75 %) so antialiasing is sampled at its midpoint. Keep the
   decoder independent of the canvas model. Report the parity error per engine and DPR after the change; if the
   WebKit paint-snap special case is no longer needed to pass, say so and remove it, otherwise keep it and say why.
2. **Then set the tolerance on measured headroom.** With a coverage edge, keep the flat 0.5 px if the worst cell leaves
   at least 0.15 px. If it does not, express the tolerance in device pixels with at least 25 % headroom over the
   measured worst and convert to CSS per DPR. Record the number either way, and state the sensitivity floor (the one
   device pixel at DPR 4 under finding 3) as a documented property of the check, not a defect.
3. **Prove the oracle still bites:** the `Times` corruption control and the uniform 11/12 model-error control must still
   fail at the new threshold. Add the DPR 4 one-device-pixel control and record whether it passes or fails, so the
   sensitivity floor is measured rather than asserted.
4. **Doc:** parity sensitivity is >= 0.4-0.5 px and the doc must not imply sub-pixel; 20.5 % is three device pixels from
   the 5 % gate but one from the 15 % target (15/11 = 14.8 %); correct the tablet row claim (the parent logo held row 1
   open across 550-1023, not only below 1024); and correct finding 6's wording — the logo sets row 1 below ~550 px at
   100 % text and ~1100 px at 200 %, which is intended, so "the logo cannot open the score row" holds only above them.
   Listing fill (57.6-59.3 %) stays a recorded follow-up.

Everything else stays. Full gate at the full width and DPR matrix, commit, do not deploy.
