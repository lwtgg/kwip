## Blocking

`MatchBanner.vue:32`: the fallback regex keeps Unicode *mark* categories with their base, but Thai `ำ` (U+0E33)
and Lao `ຳ` (U+0EB3) are category Lo — letters that Unicode's grapheme rules still attach to the preceding
character. `กำลังแข่งขัน` therefore ships as `ก` + `ำ` and paints `ก◌ำ` on the server, before hydration and with no
JS. `น้ำ`, `นำ`, `กำลัง`, `ทำ` are ordinary match words. The suite misses it because it only tests `M*` and the Thai
fixture has no `ำ`.

1. Special-case both characters in the fallback so they never start a span.
2. **Test against a real oracle, not a hand-written rule.** In `cards_browser.py`, compare our `.banner-status-word`
   boundaries with Chromium's own `Intl.Segmenter` at `granularity: 'grapheme'`: concatenating the spans must equal
   the source, and every boundary must be a grapheme boundary. That catches the next cluster case instead of a
   reviewer having to.
3. Add fixtures that would have failed: Thai with `ำ` (`กำลังแข่งขัน น้ำท่วมสนาม`), Lao (`ກຳລັງແຂ່ງຂັນ`), Devanagari
   (`दूसरा हाफ़`), Arabic (`الشوط الثاني`) and an emoji ZWJ + skin tone + flag detail. Run the oracle over all of them
   and iterate the fallback until it is green. ZWJ/emoji may keep a whole cluster unsplit; that is correct.
4. Prove red: `Array.from` again, and separately a fallback without the U+0E33/U+0EB3 case.
