and on a landscape phone the Play tap scrolls the control under the sticky header.

## 1. Start is per embed, not per mount

`playbackStarted` is set whenever any frame mounts, so a taifood mount (no button, nobody tapped) counts as
consent and the next generation's zlive embed autoplays under Save-Data.

- Replace the boolean with the embed that was started: `startedEmbed`, set to the current `heroEmbed` by a Play
  tap, cleared on a hero change.
- `held` = `saveData && zlive && startedEmbed !== heroEmbed`. A mounted zlive frame keeps its own embed, so a
  mid-session Save-Data flip never tears it down; a taifood→zlive upgrade under Save-Data is held and shows `Play`.
- Reproduce the reviewer's case as a test: Save-Data on, taifood hero mounted, refresh the feed so the same event
  gains a zlive projection — no zlive request, label `Play`; tap — mounts muted.
- Also test the inverse: Save-Data on, tap Play, refresh so the embed changes to a different zlive id — it needs
  its own Play rather than autoplaying.

## 2. The tap must leave the button usable

`scrollIntoView({ block: 'nearest' })` on the box aligns the box with `scroll-padding-top`, which puts the plate
footer — and the focused button — under the sticky header in landscape.

- After a Play tap, scroll so the button's top sits at the header clearance (`scroll-padding-top` on the root),
  and only when it is above that clearance. The box follows the button, so it is in view too.
- Assert with hit-testing, not geometry alone: at the button's centre, `document.elementFromPoint` must resolve
  into the button (or its span) after the tap, at 844×390, 667×375, 932×430 and 1280×500, and the frame must
  mount. Keep the portrait 200 % cases green.
- Do not animate; respect `prefers-reduced-motion` by leaving it instant.
