# Task 60, round four — the row sits high, and the ink oracle is the model

Read `.prompts/60-r4b.md` (the padding asymmetry) and the audit at `/tmp/60-code-review.log`. The old
`.prompts/60-r4-font-superseded.md` is kept for history but its font diagnosis is **wrong**; do not apply it.

## What the audit established

- The deployed ink alignment is fine: state vs digits is -0.69 px in Chromium, +0.13 px in WebKit, -0.63 px in
  Firefox at 1280/4x. The Acumin face declares `unicode-range: U+0030-0039`, so the *space* is excluded and the
  primary font for both the DOM and the canvas is the next family. The full-list canvas is therefore correct.
  Splitting to the first family moves the state 3.94 px off in Chromium and 25.9 px in WebKit.
- The visible defect is the row's position: 4.00 px above the score row against 8.00 px under the last tier on
  listings, 4.00 vs 16.00 on the 390 hero, 23.63 vs 35.64 on the 1280 hero, plus a 148.5 px line box for 83.2 px
  of digit ink on that hero.
- The suite's ink oracle reuses the implementation's model, so shared errors cancel. That is the process fault.
