# Task 60 round four, addendum — the row sits high in the card

The owner has looked at production and reported it directly: on the Barcelona banner and on other live banners the
logos, the score and `Live` all sit too high in the card.

Measured on production (`/tmp/60-rowprobe.log`): inside the row, `Live`, the digits and the logos are centred
together within 0.11-1.2 px, so the state nudge is not the cause of *this*. The row's **position** is: the gap
above the score row is 4.00 px while the space under the last tier is 8.00 px on listings, 16.00 px on the 390
hero and 23.63 vs 35.64 px on the 1280 hero. The asymmetry is exactly `--banner-pad-top` (4 px) against
`--banner-pad-bottom` (8 px, and `--banner-hero-pad` 16 px on the hero): the card pads less above the content than
below it.

Separately, on the 1280 hero the digit line box is 148.5 px for 83.2 px of ink because `--banner-score-leading` is
1.16 at a 128 px score, leaving 28.5 px of dead band under the digits and a 36.8 px gap to the detail.

Still do everything in `.prompts/60-r4.md` (the canvas font resolution and its guard). Then, in addition:

1. **Make the vertical padding symmetric at both scales.** Whatever the padding is above the content, it is
   below it. Keep the existing values' magnitude (listings `--spacing-2`; hero `--banner-hero-pad`) unless a
   measurement says otherwise; the change is the top matching the bottom, not a new size. Compact density keeps
   its smaller step and stays symmetric too.
2. **Trim the hero score's dead line box.** `--banner-score-leading` 1.16 at hero size is three times the
   listing's 8 px of slack. Bring the hero's line box down so the digit ink sits inside it with no more than about
   1.5x its cap height of dead space, without clipping the glyphs or the score row at 320 px. A separate
   hero-only token is fine if that is cleaner than retuning the shared one; the listing's row height must not
   change.
3. **The hero's `min-height`.** If the 1280 plate is still padded out to `--banner-hero-height` by dead space,
   say so in the doc and leave it, or lower it — measure first and record the choice.
