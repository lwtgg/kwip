# Fix round 2: three defects, all at the edges

Three, and each is real. Two of them are the same root cause: the lane is a fixed 108 px column, so a
long note wraps (making the row tall) and a zoomed numeral breaks mid-time.

1. **Wrapped notes break the proportion.** `invalid-end` and a stale row with no time measure 288×99 (2.91:1)
   and 288×91 (3.16:1) at 320, in both themes and both densities. Two-line lanes are not an exception we can
   live with: get every state to **≥3.5:1** at 320 and 390.

   Do it by shortening the note to one line, not by shrinking the target. `Availability unconfirmed` is the
   longest — find the shortest honest wording that fits at 320 comfortable and use it consistently; if a note
   still cannot fit, the lane column may grow instead of the note wrapping, but the row height must not grow.
   Keep the full sentence in the accessible label where a shortened string would lose meaning.

2. **Numerals break at 200 % text.** The lane stays 108 px and `06:44` splits into `06:4` and `4`; the time
   ends up 96 px tall on phones and 112 px on desktop. The kickoff is the one thing that must stay intact:
   stop the fact from breaking (`white-space: nowrap` on the time), let the lane column size to `max-content`
   above its floor, and let the caption and the note take the wrap instead. Verify at 320/390/1280, both
   themes, both densities, that the time renders as a single unbroken run and the facts stay legible.

3. **The test allowed it.** `tests/cards_browser.py` lowers the floor to 2.87 for a two-line note, which is
   why 2.91 passed. Remove the exception and assert 3.5 everywhere, including the wrapped-note and stale
   rows. The 200 % checks currently only look for horizontal overflow: add an assertion that the time is
   unbroken (e.g. one client rect, or its height within one line box).

Then the full matrix, contrast, CLS, 200 % text at all three widths, and the full gate. Report the before and
after for each number named above. Do not deploy, do not touch `.prompts` or `DECISIONS.md`.
